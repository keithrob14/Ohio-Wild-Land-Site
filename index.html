Welcome to Ohio Wild Land Services! My goal with OWLS is to help landowners restore and maintain native ecosystems to maximize the ecological potential regardless of the size of the property. Functional native ecosystems are increasingly rare on the modern landscape and hundreds of species of plants and animals depend on proper management in order to thrive and reproduce. Ohio consists of 95% private land, putting much of the responsibility of proper conservation in the hands of private landowners. My mission is to help these landowners create comprehensive plans to restore as much land as possible and bring wildlife habitat back to our landscape.

Services

Here are some of the main services, please make a submission in the contact section for any inquiries

Wildlife Habitat Management

I work with landowners on any scale to improve wildlife production and native diversity on the landscape. I'm a licensed herbicide applicator for invasive plant control and can work with any land maintenance project from bulldozing land clearings, to shrub/tree plantings. Watch some of our management practices here.

Residential Native Landscaping

I offer consulting and design/installation for native landscaping work and restoration of natural areas for landowners in residential and rural areas. Contact me for questions regarding native landscaping, and landscape restoration projects.

Lawn Conversions

I work with landowners to design and implement lawn conversions to improve wildlife value and decrease maintenance time and cost for landowners. No matter the size, converting your lawn is a simple yet effective way of adding ecological value back to your land.

Forest Management

I am a licensed forestry pesticide applicator and can help landowners execute forest management plans including: non-native invasive species management, timber stand improvements, woodland basal reduction, etc. Please contact me to discuss how I can help you move toward your forestry goals as a landowner.
